1. Scope

This policy explains how Reactor Clicker (the “Game”) handles information when you play on Android. The Game uses local device storage and third-party services for analytics, crash diagnostics, consent management and optional rewarded advertising. These services may collect information even though the Game does not require a player account.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and preferences locally, including energy, reactor progression, upgrades, contracts, equipment, language, audio settings and privacy-related choices. This information supports gameplay and preserves your settings between sessions. The Game does not provide a developer-operated cloud-save account. Certain gameplay milestones are recorded separately as analytics events.

You can remove local Game data through Android app storage settings. Uninstalling the Game generally removes its local data, although Android backup and restore features may restore some information depending on device settings. Removing local data does not automatically erase information already received by third-party services.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand Game usage and improve the experience. Firebase may process installation and session activity, app and device information, approximate location inferred from network information, and identifiers associated with an app installation. Standard events may include first launch, session activity, app updates and engagement.

Custom analytics events may record reactor evolutions, purchases using in-game energy, upgrade and module selections, contract completions, overheating, final protocol activation and Game completion. These events may include reactor numbers, upgrade levels and in-game identifiers. They do not intentionally include your name, email address, phone number or payment card details. Firebase Analytics collection is enabled by default in the current Game integration. Its advertising identifier collection is configured separately from the AdMob SDK.

4. Crash and technical diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to investigate crashes and certain technical failures. Reports may contain stack traces, technical error details, app/device information, session or installation identifiers, and relevant activity breadcrumbs. The Game may attach a reactor number or technical error category to help diagnose problems. Crashlytics collection is enabled by default in the current integration. Firebase Installations and Firebase Sessions support related technical functionality.

The Game is built with Unity. Depending on the final build and Unity service configuration, Unity may also process engine, performance or diagnostic information. The Game does not intentionally use legacy Unity Ads or legacy Unity Analytics for advertising or gameplay analytics.

5. Rewarded ads and consent

The Game integrates Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded advertisements. You decide whether to watch a rewarded ad; declining it does not prevent normal gameplay. Advertising requests or ad preloading may occur before you choose to watch an ad, where permitted by the applicable consent and request checks.

Google's advertising services may process IP/network information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad and app interactions, device and advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention and related operations. Even limited or non-personalized ads may involve data processing.

The Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent where applicable. Where available, the Game's Settings → Ad privacy options entry lets you revisit advertising choices. Advertising consent is separate from Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data collection.

6. Information you provide directly

The Game does not require registration, login, chat, or a payment-details form. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive information unnecessarily.

7. Third-party recipients

Information described above is processed by Google and Firebase for analytics, diagnostics, consent management and advertising. Google and participating advertising providers may process ad-related data under their own applicable policies. Unity may process technical diagnostics depending on enabled services. We may access aggregated or event-level reporting through the relevant service dashboards. Processing may occur outside your country.

8. Permissions and network access

The Game and its integrated services use internet connectivity and may use Android permissions or platform facilities related to network state, advertising identifiers, install attribution and background SDK operation. The reviewed Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise device location or external media library. Services may infer an approximate location from network information without requesting Android location permission.

9. Retention, security and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the Game, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. Analytics, advertising and diagnostic information is retained under the applicable provider settings and retention policies; retention periods vary by service and data type. Crashlytics generally applies a 90-day retention period to crash information, with deletion processing governed by Google's policies.

Google's advertising SDK documents encryption of transmitted SDK data using TLS. We rely on third-party service safeguards for information they process, but no electronic system is completely risk-free.

For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, email wyrchik.dev@gmail.com. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and the technical capabilities of the services involved. The Game does not link its installation identifiers to your email address, so we may be unable to identify a particular telemetry record using an email address alone. Clearing local Game data does not automatically request deletion from service providers.

10. Your choices and children

You can choose not to watch rewarded advertisements. Where available, you can revisit advertising consent through the Game's Ad privacy options, and Android may provide controls for advertising identifiers. The current Game Settings do not offer separate player-facing switches for Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. It does not include an age-verification flow. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the Game, contact us at wyrchik.dev@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its services or applicable requirements change. The date at the top identifies the latest published version. For privacy questions, contact wyrchik.dev@gmail.com.
